# Robotics Engineering Notes

Personal notes on the mathematical foundations and engineering practice of robotics.

## Subjects

- [Linear algebra](linear-algebra/index.md): vector spaces, matrices, decompositions, and geometry.
- [Optimization](optimization/index.md): least squares, convexity, numerical methods, and programming.
- [Control](control/index.md): linear systems, stability, and dynamical behavior.
- [Planning](planning/index.md): graph theory, game theory, and reinforcement learning.
- [Trajectory generation](trajectory-generation/index.md): path parameterization, timing, retiming, and execution.
- [Probability](probability/index.md): probability, statistics, stochastic processes, and estimation.
- [Modeling](modeling/index.md): robot dynamics and system identification.

New notes should be added to the most specific subject folder. The planning and modeling subfolders are intentionally present as an outline for future notes.
